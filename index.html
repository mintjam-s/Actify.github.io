Actify is a free online canvas and art gallery. Sketch right in your browser, or upload art you made somewhere else.

About us

Actify started with a simple idea: making art online should be easy, and sharing it should feel good.

Draw

Pick a color, pick a brush, and draw on the canvas. No downloads needed.

Upload

Made something on paper or in another app? Upload the picture and add it to your portfolio.

Share

Everything you post shows up in the gallery, where anyone can see it.

To post, draw, or upload, log in and open your Portfolio. Looking is free for everyone.
